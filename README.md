# Habitat Guide South Tyrol (offline app)

Installable offline field guide to the habitats of South Tyrol in English, Italian and German: the habitat checklist of Wilhalm et al. (2022) and the typical species after Lasen (2017).

Open the site on a phone and add it to the Home Screen; the texts and all species photos are then stored on the device.

This repository contains only the built guide. Species photos are GBIF / iNaturalist observation records used under their Creative Commons licences (resized to 360×270 px and recompressed as WebP); author, licence and source record of every photo are listed on the credits page of the app (`#credits`).
